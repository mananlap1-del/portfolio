What I changed, and why it works.

    Words used here, in plain English
    Trust strip
    A row of small badges (delivery, refund, warranty) that makes a new store feel safe.
    Buy box
    The part of the product page with the price and the Add to cart button.
    Add-to-cart rate
    Out of 100 visitors, how many tap Add to cart.
    Bounce rate
    The share of visitors who leave after looking at just one page.
    Checkout start rate
    Out of 100 visitors, how many reach the payment step.
    Scroll depth
    How far down the page people actually read.
    Objection
    A doubt that stops someone buying, like "is it worth the price?".
